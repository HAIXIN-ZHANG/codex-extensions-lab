# Skills

- [polish](polish/SKILL.md) — polish prose in its input language with Australian English defaults.
- [english-learning](../plugins/english-learning/skills/english-learning/SKILL.md) — study grammar and Australian everyday English; includes local persistence, audio and export helpers.
- [job-opportunity-search](job-opportunity-search/SKILL.md) — research and compare job opportunities on demand.
- [property-opportunity-search](property-opportunity-search/SKILL.md) — research and compare property opportunities on demand.
- [product-spike](product-spike/SKILL.md) — investigate users, markets and commercial assumptions before design.
- [product-design-brief](product-design-brief/SKILL.md) — define UX/UI flows, states and acceptance scenarios.
- [technical-design](technical-design/SKILL.md) — develop architecture, contracts and technical validation plans.
- [prompt-rewriting](prompt-rewriting/SKILL.md) — summarize and clarify rough prompts without executing them; adapted from an MIT-licensed community skill.

Each skill is independent. See [setup](../docs/setup.md) for installation and personal settings.

See [the product workflow](../docs/product-workflow.md) for how the three product-development skills fit together.
