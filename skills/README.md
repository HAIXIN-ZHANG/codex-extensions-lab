# SkillDock skills

- [skilldock-polish](skilldock-polish/SKILL.md) — polish prose in its input language with Australian English defaults.
- [skilldock-prompt-rewrite](skilldock-prompt-rewrite/SKILL.md) — summarize and clarify rough prompts without executing them; adapted from an MIT-licensed community skill.
- [skilldock-english](../plugins/skilldock-english/skills/skilldock-english/SKILL.md) — study grammar and Australian everyday English; includes local persistence, audio and export helpers.
- [skilldock-job-search](skilldock-job-search/SKILL.md) — research and compare job opportunities on demand.
- [skilldock-property-search](skilldock-property-search/SKILL.md) — research and compare property opportunities on demand.
- [skilldock-product-discovery](skilldock-product-discovery/SKILL.md) — investigate users, markets and commercial assumptions before design.
- [skilldock-ux-brief](skilldock-ux-brief/SKILL.md) — define UX/UI flows, states and acceptance scenarios.
- [skilldock-tech-design](skilldock-tech-design/SKILL.md) — develop architecture, contracts and technical validation plans.

Each skill is independent. See [setup](../docs/setup.md) for installation and personal settings.

See [the product workflow](../docs/product-workflow.md) for how the three product-development skills fit together.
