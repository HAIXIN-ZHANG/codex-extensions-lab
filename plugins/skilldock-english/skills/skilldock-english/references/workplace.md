# Workplace communication practice

Read for workplace learning or rehearsal. Adapt to the actual situation and time available; these are examples, not a required course or questionnaire.

## Choose the task

- If asked only to polish a message, return the edited message with the requested language and tone. Do not add a lesson, quiz or saved record. The separate Polish skill is appropriate when available, but is not a dependency.
- If asked why wording works, explain the relevant meaning, grammar or register, then offer a natural alternative. Preserve the intended level of certainty and the technical facts.
- For conversation practice, act as the specified audience and exchange one manageable turn at a time. Use realistic follow-up questions. Correct at a pause or when requested; allow the learner to try again. Typed practice does not establish pronunciation quality.
- For a full workplace lesson, follow the entrypoint's lesson guidance using the chosen work situation. A short meeting rehearsal remains short.

## Useful situations and language goals

| Situation | Practise |
| --- | --- |
| Stand-up or progress update | Completed work, current work, blockers and next steps without invented dates or promises |
| Requirement clarification | Paraphrasing, asking for examples, distinguishing requirements from assumptions |
| Design or code review | Explaining a tradeoff, asking about reasoning, disagreeing with a proposal respectfully |
| Demo or stakeholder update | Explaining user impact, limitations and evidence at the audience's technical level |
| Incident communication | Separating observation, hypothesis and confirmed cause; keeping uncertainty explicit |
| Asking for help or giving feedback | A concrete request, necessary context and an appropriate degree of directness |

Connect grammar where useful: tense for progress, modals for uncertainty and requests, conditionals for tradeoffs. Do not force every work situation through a grammar syllabus. A tentative estimate must remain tentative; politeness should not obscure urgency or change responsibility.

Use generic details unless the learner supplies safe context. When reusing a real work example, remove unnecessary people, customers, credentials, internal URLs and proprietary details. Keep saved work material in an authorized private destination; public learning plans require suitable public content. Do not browse confidential phrases to find a more natural translation.
