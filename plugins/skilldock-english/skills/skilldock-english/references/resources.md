# Sources and listening materials

Prefer useful existing resources over generating everything. Sources are starting points, not guarantees of current availability or proficiency fit:

- [SBS Learn English](https://www.sbs.com.au/language/english/en/learn-english): Australian life topics, podcasts, transcripts and pronunciation resources.
- [ABC Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english): everyday Australian situations and listening/video resources.
- [British Council B1–B2 grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2): explanations and interactive exercises for intermediate learners. Its [question forms lesson](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/question-forms) illustrates a test–explanation–practice sequence, though its stated level is introductory.
- [Cambridge English Grammar in Use](https://shop.cambridge.org/english/product/2700199294): British English self-study grammar for B1–B2, with explanations, examples, exercises and answers. The [supplementary exercise guide](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811084/57736/frontmatter/9781108457736_frontmatter.pdf) is a further lead; verify access and contents before relying on it. Use as a structural reference, not a source of copied exercises.
- For grammar and pronunciation, reputable dictionaries and teaching publishers; verify the relevant entry, pronunciation variety and licensing before attributing a pronunciation.
- For specialist words, original Australian equipment/manual or service pages can establish terms, not language-teaching or safety authority.

Search by the learner's actual topic and level. Open the selected page, record title/direct URL and date checked, and explain why it is useful. A search snippet is discovery, not verification. Link original audio/video when suitable; do not invent downloadable files or bypass access restrictions. Distinguish an audio recording actually heard from a transcript or description only read.

Use links, short compliant excerpts and original explanations/lesson exercises. Public availability does not authorize copying full articles, transcripts or third-party audio into this open-source repository or a redistributed pack. Cache source metadata and the assistant's own notes by default. Only include third-party media with appropriate rights; synthesized original examples should be labeled as synthesized. Preserve source status when revising a lesson.
