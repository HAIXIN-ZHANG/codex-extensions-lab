# Property intelligence workspace

Use the existing property-search skill for on-demand searches. Treat research as time-specific: distinguish advertised prices, observations, transactions and forecasts, and record sources and dates. Missing facts remain unknown. Never treat a missing listing as a sale or cancellation.

Keep personal preferences, financial information, private research history and database files outside this public repository. Do not contact agents, register, buy reports, change access or schedule research without authorization. Preserve prior observations; do not overwrite history or import stale observations. Check source terms and robots rules before any automated collection; no automated collector is provided here.

The scripts are tools, not an investment recommendation. Confirm any tax, financing or property-specific inputs before relying on calculations.
